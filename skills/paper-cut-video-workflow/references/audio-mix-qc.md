# Audio Mix And QC

## Music

- Choose light, tactile, handmade, playful, or warm music unless the product calls for another mood.
- Keep music supportive; narration remains the anchor.
- Leave the opening hook audible and the CTA clean.

## SFX

Use motivated sound effects only:

- paper slide
- page flip
- scissors snip
- soft pop
- stamp
- tape pull
- whoosh
- tiny impact

## Ducking

- Lower music under narration.
- Reduce SFX where they mask consonants.
- Fade music and ambience at start/end.
- Avoid sudden loud transition sounds on mobile speakers.

## Human Listening Check

- Can every word be understood on phone speakers?
- Does music fight the voice?
- Are brand names pronounced correctly?
- Is the CTA easy to hear?
