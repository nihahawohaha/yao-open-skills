# Delivery Checklist

## Final Package

- Final master video.
- Platform-optimized export.
- Preview/compressed export.
- Cover frame.
- Subtitle file when used.
- Narration audio.
- Music and SFX license/source notes.
- Layered PNG assets.
- Keyframe images.
- Prompt log.
- Project/source files.
- QC report.
- Revision notes.
- Privacy/voice-rights note.

## Revision Categories

- Script: message, wording, CTA, claims.
- Visual: style, palette, product accuracy, text.
- Timing: shot length, pacing, hook, CTA.
- Voice: pronunciation, emotion, speed, rights.
- Audio: music, SFX, ducking, loudness.
- Export: format, aspect ratio, compression, subtitles.
