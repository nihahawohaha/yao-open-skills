# Final QC

## Technical QC

- Resolution matches target platform.
- FPS is consistent, usually 30 fps.
- Video codec is H.264 or required delivery codec.
- Audio codec is AAC/WAV as required.
- No black frames, frozen accidental frames, or missing layers.
- Text stays inside platform safe areas.
- No clipped audio or obvious noise.
- File opens and decodes fully.

## Picture QC

- Product is recognizable.
- Paper-cut style is consistent across shots.
- Shadows and layer depth feel coherent.
- Text is readable on mobile.
- No prompt artifacts, warped logos, broken hands, or unreadable labels.

## Story QC

- Hook lands within 3 seconds.
- Viewer understands the product promise.
- Visuals support narration.
- Pacing is neither rushed nor empty.
- CTA is clear.
