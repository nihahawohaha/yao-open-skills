## Clarify Before Production

- What must the viewer remember after 3 seconds?
- What product detail must appear visually?
- Is the goal awareness, conversion, education, or brand feeling?
- Are there approved brand colors, fonts, slogans, or legal lines?
- Is any reference voice user-owned or explicitly authorized?
