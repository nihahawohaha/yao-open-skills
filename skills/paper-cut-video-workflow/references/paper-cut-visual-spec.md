# Paper-Cut Visual Spec

## Core Look

- Handmade paper collage, visible fibers, slight cut-edge irregularity.
- Layered 2.5D depth with soft directional shadows.
- Simple shapes, clear silhouettes, tactile materials.
- Stop-motion inspired movement with small imperfect offsets.
- Warm but balanced palette; do not let the entire video become one color family.

## Shot Design

- Keep product or subject readable in the first viewport.
- Use foreground frames, midground product/action, and background texture.
- Reserve strong contrast for product, hook, and CTA.
- Use text as cut-paper labels or stamped notes, not generic floating captions.

## Prompt Keywords

- paper cutout, layered paper collage, fiber texture, handmade edges
- soft cast shadow, tactile paper, stop-motion frame feel
- clean silhouette, editorial craft animation, transparent background layer

## Negative Prompt Ideas

- plastic 3D render, glossy CGI, flat vector icon, blurry stock photo
- hyperreal skin, messy typography, unreadable text, heavy gradients
- dark low-detail background, excessive particles, distorted product
