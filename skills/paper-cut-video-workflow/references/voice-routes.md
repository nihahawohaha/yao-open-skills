# Voice Routes

## Route Selection

- IndexTTS-2: choose for local/private voice generation, Chinese voice style control, and user-owned reference voice.
- MiniMax: choose for fast Chinese commercial narration and simple web/API production.
- ElevenLabs: choose for multilingual, polished narration, or broad voice library needs.
- Human recording: choose when performance, legality, or brand trust matters more than automation.

## Safety Rules

- Do not clone, imitate, or synthesize a real person's voice without explicit rights or consent.
- Do not store reference voice or private generated voice inside this skill.
- Keep voice source, consent note, transcript, generated audio, and model settings in the project folder.
- When rights are unclear, use a neutral synthetic voice or request user-owned recording.

## Narration Prep

- Clean the script into short spoken lines.
- Avoid tongue-twisters and dense numbers.
- Export a pronunciation note for brand names, product names, and uncommon terms.
- Generate WAV for editing, then MP3/AAC only for delivery when needed.
